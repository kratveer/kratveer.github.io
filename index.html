Kratveer Singh

I am currently a PhD student in the Department of Optics at Palacky University Olomouc. My research focus is in experimental realization of the light-matter interaction at single photon - single phonon level in trapped ions.

Discipline

Trapped Ions, Quantum State Engineering, Quantum Information Science

Education

2022 - Present Ph.D. Student at Palacky University Olomouc, Czech Republic
2014 - 2020 BSMS at Indian Institute of Science Education and Research Bhopal

Current Research

Exploring quantum communication, quantum privacy, and quantum correlation measures.
