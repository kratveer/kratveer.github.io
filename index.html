01 / ABOUT

Education

I am currently a PhD student in the Department of Optics at Palacký University Olomouc. My research focuses on the experimental realization of light–matter interactions at the single-photon / single-phonon level in trapped ions.

My broader interests lie at the intersection of experimental quantum optics, trapped-ion physics, quantum state engineering and quantum information science.

Research interests

TRAPPED ION SYSTEMS

Quantum state engineering

QUANTUM OPTICS
